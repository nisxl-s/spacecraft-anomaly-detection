# Sustained record transfer check

Freeze the original NASA two-hit, 30-reading cap and five-low rearm policy and its uncapped two-hit records. Use ESA Mission 2 v2 channels 15–20 selected consecutively after the six previously analyzed targets, provided metadata confirms these are noncategorical targets. Exclude previously studied incident IDs from scoring. Use the official temporal split, hourly data and the absolute first difference as a fixed persistence-residual reference score, with a training-only 99th-percentile threshold. This evaluates the policy on a different source and score, not transfer of fitted NASA LSTM weights. An invalid or missing hour resets both policies. Use official anomaly intervals only with fully observed hourly bins from start through end. Assign only event-overlap credit and compare same-channel events. Freeze alarm output before reading selected event annotations. Require exact paging parity, at least one additional detected labeled channel-event, and a maximum 10% active record occupancy on pooled eligible hours. The 10% is a provisional analysis budget.
